Buying a scent

Replace uncertainty with a discovery experience customers can feel good about.

0.6% → 1.1%Overall conversion rate
Inside the work +

The frictionChoosing an unfamiliar fragrance online made the first purchase feel risky.

The workClearer product information, easier discovery, and a sample set with a voucher towards the next full-size bottle.

The learningA discount alone showed no clear improvement in the portfolio. The redeemable discovery set addressed the decision itself and reported a 51% conversion-rate lift in its own test.

FABCURATEFASHION / ECOMMERCE

Less hesitation.
More completed carts.

Answer the questions that stand between “I like it” and “I’ll buy it.”

1.2% → 1.7%Overall conversion rate
Inside the work +

The frictionShoppers needed clearer product benefits, easier fabric quantity selection, and reassurance before paying.

The workCustomer research informed product information. Iterations improved length selection. The cart surfaced savings, delivery and return information.

The learningThe first quantity-selection test improved add-to-cart without a major purchase impact. A retest reported a 9% conversion-rate lift. The separate cart test reported a 16% lift.

Source: portfolio, slides 11–14. Overall results and individual test lifts are separate measures.
